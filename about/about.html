History

I was sitting in Electrical Class learning the math formulas. I got tired of having to remeber all the different formulas and I thought to myself, "Why doesn't someone come up with a calculator that has all of these formulas in it."

I had remebered that I wanted to learn how to do computer programming, so I thought to myself, "Why don't I create the calculator.", and so the Massive Calculator was born.

Future of The Massive Calculator

The future for the Massive Calculator is to have versions available for smartphone downloads and to have every mathematical formula known to man.
